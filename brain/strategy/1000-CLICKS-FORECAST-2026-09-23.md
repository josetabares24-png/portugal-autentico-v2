## THE SCALE OF 1,000 CLICKS/DAY

1,000 organic clicks/day = roughly:
- **28,000 clicks per 28 days**
- **365,000 clicks/year**

Compared with the latest 28-day baseline:
- current: 49 clicks / 28d
- target: 28,000 clicks / 28d
- required multiplier: **~571x**

Compared with the latest 7-day run rate:
- current: 2.14 clicks/day
- target: 1,000 clicks/day
- required multiplier: **~467x**

This is not a near-term SEO optimization target. It requires a materially larger publication and stronger authority.

## IMPRESSION REQUIREMENT

## PLANNING FORECAST

### Aggressive case: ~30–36 months
Requires:
- ~20% monthly click growth for years;
- several high-volume clusters working;
- strong page-1 penetration;
- backlinks / citations / brand searches growing;
- editorial production capacity increasing;
- no major platform/search disruption;
- repeatable content discovery beyond today's ~60-article footprint.

This is possible, but should be treated as an upside case.

### Base case: ~42–60 months
Planning assumption:
- periods of 12–15% compound growth;
- pauses/plateaus;
- some failed experiments;
- gradual authority growth;
- stronger CTR;
- systematic updates;
- a larger but still selective editorial library.

**Working planning estimate: around 4 years, with a realistic range of 3.5–5 years.**

### Conservative case: ~60–84 months
If growth averages closer to 8–10% monthly or authority builds slowly, the target moves into roughly 5–7 years.

## WHAT WOULD MAKE THE FORECAST FASTER

- multiple pages reaching top 3 for meaningful queries;
- clusters where one success lifts several related pages;
- earned links/mentions from travel, media, institutions or partners;
- brand searches and direct/repeat usage;
- editorial pieces that earn distribution outside search;
- successful English/Portuguese expansion **only if evidence later supports it**;
- scalable maintenance so old pages stay accurate;
- a small editorial/production team rather than one person doing everything.

## WHAT WOULD MAKE IT SLOWER

- publishing low-demand articles;
- rewriting the same pages repeatedly;
- weak authority / no links;
- traffic concentrated in tiny long-tail queries;
- seasonal dependence;
- generic AI-style content;
- stale operational information;
- adding product surfaces that distract from acquisition;
- measuring activity instead of traffic and revenue.

## DECISION

**1,000 clicks/day is the long-term north star, not the current operating KPI.**
