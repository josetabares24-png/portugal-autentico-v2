## Why these six

They were selected before this pass because they had:
- independent Search Console signal;
- dynamic facts likely to change;
- or measurable commercial value.

## Method

For each page:
1. inspect production data object;
2. check current official/institutional source;
3. remove claims that were unsupported, stale or framed as invented local expertise;
4. update `officialUrl` / `lastVerified`;
5. preserve URL and product role;
6. avoid unrelated layout or CTA changes.

## Product lesson

For practical city content, "fresh" is not a date label.

Fresh means:
- the operational claim was checked;
- the source is known;
- uncertainty is stated;
- stale local folklore is removed.

This is the visitor-impact update discipline identified in the European benchmark.
