# Content strategy

## Principle

The site already has a meaningful editorial base. Growth should come first from **stronger existing pages + coherent clusters**, not mass publishing.

## Editorial quality rules

- Answer the search intent early.
- Distinguish stable facts from changing details.
- Date or source volatile information.
- Do not invent local experience.
- Do not use "secret", "authentic", "best", "locals do X" as factual claims without evidence.
- Avoid generic filler sections added only for word count.
- Each article should link to the next genuinely useful article, not just a random related post.

## Internal-link rule

Every priority article should have:
- at least one meaningful link from a stronger/related page;
- useful outgoing links within its cluster;
- anchors that describe the destination naturally.

Do not manufacture dozens of repetitive exact-match anchors.

## Publishing cadence

No fixed quota.

Publish only when:
- the content gate in [[03-SEO-DECISION-ENGINE]] is satisfied;
- existing high-opportunity pages are not being neglected;
- the new page has a planned place in the cluster.

Quality and measured demand beat volume.
