# Mission

## What Estaba en Lisboa is becoming

A focused Spanish-language editorial publication about Lisbon and Portugal that wins useful organic searches, earns trust and converts that attention into economically useful actions.

**Blog-first. Metrics-first. No feature theatre.**

The project is not trying to become a large tourism portal right now.

## North star

Build a compounding loop:

**Search demand → excellent answer → organic click → useful reading → next useful action → revenue signal → measured learning**

## Current milestone

Reach and sustain **28,000+ Google Search impressions in a finalized 28-day window** (about 1,000/day).

This is a growth milestone, not the business goal. Clicks, CTR, engagement and monetization must improve with it.

## Staged target

1. Stabilize/recover 300 impressions/day.
2. Sustain 500/day.
3. Sustain 750/day.
4. Sustain 1,000/day.

Do not optimize for one-day spikes.

## Product direction

Keep the public experience increasingly editorial:
- articles;
- editorial hubs/categories;
- essential trust/legal pages;
- contextual monetization inside useful content.

Freeze feature expansion until traffic and conversion evidence justify it.

## What we do not optimize for

- redesigns because they look more modern;
- adding tools because they are interesting;
- publishing volume for its own sake;
- PageSpeed scores detached from real bottlenecks;
- vanity rankings for irrelevant queries;
- affiliate clicks that damage usefulness or trust.

## Decision filter

A proposed change should improve or protect at least one of:

- impressions;
- query coverage;
- position;
- organic CTR;
- organic clicks;
- engaged reading;
- useful internal navigation;
- affiliate/conversion rate;
- technical crawl/index reliability.

If it improves none of these, it is not a priority.
